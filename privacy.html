TV Launcher privacy policy

TV Launcher is a personal home screen app for Android TV. This page explains what it does with your Google data.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (read-only): your subscriptions, to show new videos from channels you follow.
  • Google Photos (photos you pick): only the photos you choose in the Google Photos picker, to show behind the home screen and in the screensaver.

Where it goes

Everything stays on your TV. Sign-in tokens and the photo copies you pick are stored only in the app's private storage on the TV. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing it

In the launcher's settings, "Sign out of YouTube" and "Remove Google Photos from this TV" delete the tokens and photo copies and revoke the TV's access. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TV Launcher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
